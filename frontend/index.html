“We believe in a future that is automated and telemetry-driven, which is why we have chosen to eliminate parking latency. Our ambition is to modernize urban transit by making real-time bay allocation easy and accessible through ultrasonic IoT mesh and automated ANPR.”

1

Real-Time Spatial Visibility

Finding parking manually wastes time, congests cities, and burns fuel. We believe drivers deserve zero search latency with live ultrasonic bay telemetry, clear occupancy status, and instant frictionless navigation across multi-tier parking facilities.

2

Security & Encryption

We believe in privacy-first urban infrastructure. Slotify encrypts Aadhaar documents with AES-256-GCM before storing them in MongoDB GridFS. Authenticated endpoints restrict access to the account that uploaded each document.

3

Driver & Facility
Centricity

We build for both urban commuters and facility managers. By automating barrier gates via FASTag/RFID and providing granular telemetry analytics, we eliminate bottlenecks and maximize parking space utilization with effortless design.
